# Contract Lifecycle

An eligible contract must satisfy the point-in-time lifecycle rule for date `T`: listed by `T`, not delisted by `T`, not matured by `T`, and marked tradable when that field is available. Missing lifecycle evidence is `unresolved`, not eligible by default.

The provider dominant mapping is diagnostic evidence. Internal migration requires the prior confirmed contract and an active Top-K candidate pool ranked by within-instrument volume and open-interest percentile scores.
